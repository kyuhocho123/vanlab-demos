What to tell Claude (2 minutes)
  1. Which style would you want the game to look like: Technical, Realistic, Cozy, or none of them?
  2. Is there something in another style you would keep?
  3. Try Day and Evening in your favourite. Which mood is closer to what you imagine?
  4. What looks wrong or cheap to you? Name the worst thing.

This is a direction test, not final art. There are no model or image files here; the question is whether art made only by code can get good enough.
