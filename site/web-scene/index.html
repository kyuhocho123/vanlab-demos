  1. Note the frames/s number. Press “+500 boxes” until it drops below your screen's top speed; note how many boxes that took.
  2. Put a finger on an item and drag. Does it move on the first touch, every time?
  3. Does the item stay under your finger, or lag behind?
  4. Drag empty space to look around; pinch to zoom, also with one finger starting on an item. Does it ever grab an item by mistake?
  5. Overall feel, 1 to 5.
